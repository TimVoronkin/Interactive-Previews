Privacy Policy for Interactive Previews

Last updated: May 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the browser extension Interactive Previews.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Interactive Previews does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or personally identifiable information. All operations (such as resolving links, displaying image/PDF previews, and managing settings) are performed entirely locally within your browser context.

2. Permissions

  • storage: Used solely to save your extension configuration (e.g. whitelist/blacklist domains, size settings, and active formats) locally on your device.
  • Host permissions (<all_urls>): Used to detect previewable media links (images/PDFs) on websites you visit and to fetch PDF document bytes and image metadata directly in your browser. This data never leaves your device.

3. Data Transfer and Third Parties

No user data is ever sold, leased, or transferred to third-party entities. The extension does not communicate with any external tracking, analytics, or remote servers.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

5. Contact

If you have any questions, you can open an issue on the official GitHub repository: github.com/TimVoronkin/Interactive-Previews.
